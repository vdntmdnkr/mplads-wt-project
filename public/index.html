What is MPLADS?

The Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS) is a Government of India programme that enables Members of Parliament to recommend development work in their constituencies with an annual entitlement of ₹5 crore per MP.

Works recommended cover roads, community buildings, drinking water supply, health facilities, education infrastructure, and disaster relief — executed by district authorities and monitored by the Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI).
